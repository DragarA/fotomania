Design System:

    Warm neutral color palette: soft stone grays (#fafaf9, #f5f5f4, #e7e5e4) with sophisticated stone-800 (#292524) accents
    Ultra-thin typography (font-weight: 300) with increased letter-spacing (0.01em - 0.03em)
    Minimal 4px rounded corners (rounded, not rounded-lg)
    Ultra-light borders (opacity: 0.06-0.08) and subtle shadows
    Generous whitespace (px-10, py-8, space-y-8)

UI Elements:

    Borderless white cards on stone-50 backgrounds
    Thin stroke icons (strokeWidth={1.5})
    Refined buttons with tracking-widest and uppercase text-xs
    Subtle hover states with gentle transitions (duration-500, duration-700)
    Minimalist loading states with thin border spinners
    Floating CTA buttons with subtle shadows

Visual Hierarchy:

    Clear but elegant spacing ratios
    Airy layouts with balanced negative space
    Editorial photo grid with refined hover effects
    Sophisticated color transitions and scale effects
    Clean, high-end aesthetic throughout

The entire application now has an elevated, magazine-quality feel that's far from the typical Tailwind starter look, with every interaction feeling smooth and refined.
